Music Data Analysis Dashboard Report

This Power BI dashboard provides an analysis of music track features such as popularity, energy, danceability, and genre distribution. The visualizations help identify trends across artists and genres.

1. Top Genres by Average Popularity
The acoustic genre has the highest average popularity among the analyzed tracks.
The afrobeat genre follows but with lower average popularity.
2. Key Metrics
Average Danceability: 0.61
Tracks generally have a moderate level of danceability.
Average Energy: 0.57
Energy levels are moderate across tracks, indicating a balanced mix of calm and energetic songs.
Average Popularity: 33.45
Popularity scores suggest room for identifying trending tracks.
3. Average Popularity by Artist
Top artists by average popularity include:
Jason Mraz
Caleb Santos
Calle 13 (Silvio Rodríguez)
Ray LaMontagne
Anna Nalick
These artists consistently have tracks with higher popularity metrics.
4. Popularity vs Energy Scatter Plot
The scatter plot shows the relationship between energy and popularity for the acoustic and afrobeat genres.
Observation:
Tracks with moderate energy levels tend to have higher popularity.
Acoustic tracks (light blue) dominate the higher popularity range.
Afrobeat tracks (dark blue) are clustered at lower popularity scores.
5. Popularity by Genre
Pie chart breakdown:
Acoustic: 63.51% of total popularity contribution.
Afrobeat: 36.49% of total popularity contribution.
Acoustic tracks are contributing more significantly to overall popularity.
6. Danceability Distribution
The distribution histogram shows that most tracks have danceability between 0.4 and 0.8.
Indicates that moderately danceable tracks are the most common in the dataset.
Summary

The dashboard highlights that acoustic tracks dominate both in popularity and contribution to the dataset, while afrobeat tracks have lower popularity but still contribute a significant portion. Energy and danceability are moderately distributed, and certain artists consistently outperform others in terms of popularity.

This analysis can be used to:

Identify popular artists and genres.
Guide music curation or playlist recommendations.
